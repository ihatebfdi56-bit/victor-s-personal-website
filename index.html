ABOUT THE DEEP

A world hidden beneath the waves.

The deep sea is one of Earth's most mysterious environments. Sunlight disappears as depth increases, pressure rises dramatically, and many animals have evolved unusual ways to survive.

Some deep-sea animals make their own light through bioluminescence. Others use enormous mouths, sensitive tentacles, camouflage, or unusual body shapes to find food and avoid predators.

This site is a collection of deep-sea themed words, ideas, creatures, and projects—built around the weird and wonderful life found far below the ocean surface.

CREATURES OF THE ABYSS

Deep Sea Life

Meet some of the bizarre animals that make the deep ocean feel like an alien world.

01
GI

CRUSTACEAN • INDONESIA

Giant Isopod

An enormous relative of the familiar woodlouse. The species Bathynomus raksasa was described from deep waters off southern Java, making it a particularly cool Indonesian deep-sea find.

02
DS

CEPHALOPOD • JAPAN / PACIFIC

Dumbo Octopus

These deep-sea octopuses swim by flapping ear-like fins on their mantles. They can hover above the seafloor and live at remarkable depths, making them one of the deep ocean's strangest gentle giants.

03
MF

CEPHALOPOD • ABYSSAL

Bigfin Squid

Magnapinna squids are famous for their extremely long, delicate-looking arms and tentacles. Their bizarre posture makes them look almost like floating alien machinery.

04
PG

JELLYFISH • DEEP PELAGIC

Phantom Jelly

The giant phantom jelly, Stygiomedusa gigantea, has enormous ribbon-like oral arms. It is rarely seen, which makes every confirmed encounter feel like a glimpse into another world.

05
CE

FISH • JAPAN / DEEP SEA

Chimaera

Chimaeras are ghostly relatives of sharks and rays. Their unusual faces, tapering bodies, and deep-water lifestyle give them an ancient-looking appearance unlike most modern fish.
